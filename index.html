Grandpa Kolev and the Very Small Incident

A dry-humour comic in 5 pages. The jokes are on the bureaucracy, never the victims.

Page 1: “Everything Was Under Control”

ANDREIGrandpa, school project! What really happened at Chernobyl?
KOLEVNothing. Everything was under control.
ANDREIThat’s literally what the textbook says you said.
Pripyat, 1986. Population 49,000. Free apartments, fresh oranges, a brand-new Ferris wheel. Paradise, with paperwork.
KOLEVBest job in the USSR. They sold oranges in April.
April 26. Night shift.
KOLEVThey ran a “safety test.” It involved switching off the safety systems.
ANDREITo test safety?
KOLEVSoviet logic, kid.

Page 2: The Roof Gets a Skylight

01:23. Reactor 4.
KOLEVThen Reactor 4 got a skylight.
ANDREIYou mean it exploded.
KOLEVI mean it got a skylight. Officially.
Official radiation reading: 3.6.
KOLEVSir, the meter’s maximum is 3.6.
OFFICIALThen it is 3.6. Meeting adjourned.
Next morning, Pripyat: weddings, football, school, sunbathing. Nobody was told anything.
KOLEVPanic was successfully prevented. So was information.

Page 3: Please Pack for Three Days

April 27, about 36 hours later. 1,200 buses.
OFFICIALBring documents and food for three days.
KOLEVI packed for three days. It has been forty years.
April 28. Sweden’s own plant sets off alarms. Sweden checks, then looks east.
STATE TVAn accident occurred. Measures are being taken. Thank you.
KOLEVShort announcement. About a sneeze long.
May 1. Kyiv holds the May Day parade, as planned.
KOLEVNobody told the clouds to cancel.

Page 4: The Part That Isn’t Funny

About 600,000 “liquidators” cleaned up. Reward: a certificate and a firm handshake.
KOLEVThey sent robots to clear the roof. The robots broke. So they sent people. Cheaper, apparently.
ANDREIThat’s awful.
KOLEVThat’s management.
Over 350,000 people were relocated. New flats, new neighbours, old stigma.
KOLEVThe neighbours thought we glowed.
ANDREIDo you?
KOLEVOnly at parties.
Thyroid cancers in children rose. Most were treated. The UN later called mental health the biggest impact.
KOLEVDoctors saved them, not press releases. And worry did the rest, when nobody tells you anything.

Page 5: Forty Years Later

The 30 km Exclusion Zone. Humans: banned. Wolves: no objections.
KOLEVNature took over. Wolves, deer, lynx. Excellent tenants. No complaints about the heating.
A few hundred grandmothers moved back illegally.
OFFICIALPlease leave.
BABUSHKANo.
OFFICIALOkay.
Today: tourists, selfies, and a Ferris wheel that never opened (planned opening: May 1, 1986).
ANDREISo what’s the lesson?
KOLEVTell the truth early. Measure properly. Never trust a safety test that starts by disabling safety.
ANDREIPutting that in my project.
KOLEVWrite: “Grandpa was right.”

THE END. Please remain calm. Everything is under control.
Based on real events (1986 to today), simplified for comic effect. Dialogue is invented; the characters are fictional.
